The guiding rule behind the fallback scores

A category's fallback score is never lower than its own mildest listed issue. If we genuinely don't know exactly what's wrong, the system assumes it's at least as serious as the mildest known thing that can go wrong in that trade — never less.

Fallback score, by category

This is the score used only when the specific issue isn't in the list. The grey bar shows where the category's own listed issues fall — the marker is the fallback, which always sits at or above that range.

A known limitation

This rule protects against the mildest known issue in a category, not the worst. Utilities & Supply spans a mild "Smart meter installation" (65) up to a severe "Gas supply issue" (130) — the fallback can only be as cautious as the mildest of those. The lasting protection against that gap is keeping the specific-issue list comprehensive enough that "unlisted" is rarely picked for something genuinely serious.
