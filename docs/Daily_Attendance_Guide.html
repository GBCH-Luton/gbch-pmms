2 Allow location when asked

Your phone will ask for permission to share your location. This is normal — it's how the app records where you clocked in from.

  • Always tap Allow, not "Don't Allow".
  • If you accidentally block it, clocking in won't work — you'll need to turn location on in your phone's Settings and try again.

3 You're in for the day

Now you can see your jobs as normal. Notice the bar at the top — that's your reminder that you're clocked in, and it stays there all day.

  • If you clocked in after 9:00am, this bar turns orange and shows exactly how many minutes late it was (see the second screen alongside) — that's just for your manager to see, nothing you need to do.
  • Tap into a job and clock in/out of it exactly like you always have — that part hasn't changed.

4 Popping out? Tell the app first

Going to buy materials, or taking your lunch break? Tap "Leaving Site" on the banner before you go.

  • Choose Buying Materials or Lunch Break.
  • If it's a materials run, type the shop name in the box — e.g. "B&Q Luton".
  • Tap Start.
  • If you're mid-way through a job when you leave, the app quietly notes which job it was — your manager can see that later, you don't need to type it yourself.
👉 Pick a reason, add a note, tap Start

6 End of day — clock out

Once you've finished all your jobs, tap "Clock Out for the Day" on the banner.

  • If you still have a job showing "In Progress", it'll stop you and tell you to pause or complete that job first.
  • If you're still marked "Away", it'll ask you to tap "I'm Back" first.
⚠ Finish or pause your job (and tap "I'm Back" if away) before clocking out

7 Clocking out early? Give a reason

If you tap "Clock Out for the Day" before 5:00pm, the app asks why first — same idea as the late flag in the morning, just the other end of the day.

  • Type a short reason — a sentence is plenty.
  • This only appears if you're finishing early. Clocking out at or after 5:00pm doesn't ask for anything.
⚠ A reason is required before an early clock-out goes through

8 You're done for the day

You're back at this same screen — that's normal, it just means you're successfully clocked out. You won't see your jobs again until you clock in tomorrow.

If you needed to come back on for any reason (forgot something, asked to help with an urgent job), just tap "Clock In for the Day" again — it works the same way, any time of day.

1 Today's Attendance table

Open Clocking in the admin sidebar. The first section shows every builder, whether they've clocked in yet, and what they're doing right now:

  • Available — clocked in, not on a job or away
  • On Job #N — actively working a ticket
  • Travelling: [note] or On break — purple, currently away
  • Off shift — hasn't clocked in for the day

Late clock-ins and early clock-outs are both flagged in amber, each with the reason given (a required reason for early leaves, an automatic minute count for lateness).

2 "Where's the Team" on the Dashboard

The Dashboard home page now has this panel sitting next to the Daily Briefing — a quicker, at-a-glance version of Today's Attendance, for the whole portfolio in one place.

  • Filter to one builder with the dropdown, top right.
  • If a "left site"/"returned" entry happened mid-job, the job number shows next to it and is clickable straight through to that ticket in Pipeline.
  • The list scrolls on its own once it fills up — it won't push the rest of the dashboard down.

3 Someone can't clock in themselves? Override it

Dead phone, no signal, whatever the reason — every row has a "Clock In For Them" / "Clock Out For Them" button on the right.

  • You must type a short reason — it's kept on record against that shift.
  • The row shows "Manager override" afterwards so it's clear it wasn't self-reported.

Further down the same page, Monthly Hours adds up every clocked day for whichever month you pick.

6 Adjusting the rules

Under Settings → Daily Attendance, you control both times used everywhere above:

  • Clock-in deadline — clocking in after this time gets flagged late.
  • Clock-out reminder — the same time works both ways: anyone still clocked in past it gets a push reminder, and anyone clocking out before it has to give a reason.

7 Per-job clocking still works the same

Nothing about clocking in/out of an individual job — or the Completed Job Timesheet, mileage, estimates — has changed. Daily Attendance sits above that as a separate, whole-day layer, not a replacement for it.
