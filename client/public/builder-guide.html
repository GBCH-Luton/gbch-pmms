How a Builder Uses the App

Exactly what a builder sees on their phone, step by step, from clocking in for the day to clocking out.

Follow these steps in order — this is exactly what a builder sees on their phone from the moment they open the app in the morning to the moment they finish for the day.

1 Open the app

This is the first thing a builder sees every morning — before their jobs, before anything else. They can't get past this screen without clocking in.

  • It greets them by name and says "good morning" or "good afternoon" depending on the time.
  • They tap the big teal button to start their day.
👉 Tap "Clock In for the Day"

2 Allow location when asked

Their phone asks for permission to share location. This is normal — it's how the app records where they clocked in from.

  • They should always tap Allow, not "Don't Allow".
  • If they accidentally block it, clocking in won't work — they'll need to turn location on in their phone's Settings and try again.

3 They're in for the day

Now they can see their jobs as normal. Notice the bar at the top — that's their reminder that they're clocked in, and it stays there all day.

  • If they clocked in after 9:00am, this bar turns orange and shows exactly how many minutes late it was (see the second screen alongside) — that's just for a manager to see, nothing the builder needs to do.
  • Tapping into a job and starting work on it is a separate step of its own — see next.

4 Starting a job

From the job list, they tap a job, then say where they're travelling from (or "Already on site" for a second job at the same property) and log the miles driven, if any.

  • Tap "I've arrived — start work" to begin.
  • The job screen then shows a live running timer — that's the system logging exactly when work started on this specific job, separate from the day-level clock-in.
  • Only one job can run at a time — they finish or pause the current one before starting another.
👉 Pick where you're coming from, log miles, tap "I've arrived"

5 Popping out? Tell the app first

Going to buy materials, or taking a lunch break? They tap "Leaving Site" on the banner before going.

  • Choose Buying Materials or Lunch Break.
  • If it's a materials run, they type the shop name in the box — e.g. "B&Q Luton".
  • Then tap Start.
  • If they're mid-way through a job when they leave, the app quietly notes which job it was — a manager can see that later, the builder doesn't need to type it themselves.
👉 Pick a reason, add a note, tap Start

7 End of day — clock out

Once they've finished all their jobs, they tap "Clock Out for the Day" on the banner.

  • If they still have a job showing "In Progress", it stops them and tells them to pause or complete that job first.
  • If they're still marked "Away", it asks them to tap "I'm Back" first.
⚠ Finish or pause the job (and tap "I'm Back" if away) before clocking out

8 Clocking out early? Give a reason

If they tap "Clock Out for the Day" before 5:00pm, the app asks why first — same idea as the late flag in the morning, just the other end of the day.

  • They type a short reason — a sentence is plenty.
  • This only appears if finishing early. Clocking out at or after 5:00pm doesn't ask for anything.
⚠ A reason is required before an early clock-out goes through

9 Done for the day

They're back at this same screen — that's normal, it just means they're successfully clocked out. They won't see their jobs again until they clock in tomorrow.

If they need to come back on for any reason (forgot something, asked to help with an urgent job), tapping "Clock In for the Day" again works the same way, any time of day.
