3 Tap "Start Job" → the screen locks

The main screen is replaced by the timer: a big running clock counting up from the moment they started, a small line above it showing the estimated time for this specific job, and one button — Stop.

  • The header and ☰ menu stay reachable — this isn't a total lock, see the Open Questions tab for why.
  • What's hidden is anything job-related: the job list, Available Jobs, raising a new ticket. Team Chat, Metrics, Mileage stay open.
  • The estimate is whatever's already set on the ticket (Estimated Time, used elsewhere in PMMS already) — not a new field.

4 Tap Stop → one dropdown, six options

Replaces today's three separate actions (Complete / Pause / Couldn't get access) with a single control:

  • Job Completed — goes to the existing note + photo completion screen.
  • Waiting for Materials — asks one more question first. It's not one thing — see steps 5 & 6, and step 7.
  • Going to the Office / Lunch Break — a short personal trip, timed. See step 6.
  • Unable to Do the Job — job pauses, note required, flags the new manager metric (see Manager tab). Started as optional; changed to required once it was clear an optional note made this the easiest zero-friction way to drop any job.
  • Other — note becomes required, not optional.

6 Going myself / Going to the Office / Lunch Break → still locked, timed

All three are the same mechanic: a short, specific trip the builder is personally making. The lock stays on (menu still reachable, same as step 3), but switches to a second, smaller timer counting exactly how long they're away, with one button: Resume Job. Tapping it un-freezes the original job timer and returns to step 3.

This is the actual point of the whole feature — before, a materials run just disappeared into "logged after the fact"; now it's timed to the minute, automatically, tied to the exact job it was for.

Added after launch: if one of these breaks runs past a configurable threshold (default 45 minutes) without being resumed, the builder's manager gets an alert too — see Manager tab, step 5.

9 The list reappears — nearby jobs get a nudge, not a takeover

Whenever the job list becomes reachable again (Job Completed, Unable to Do, or a materials wait dropped to On Hold), it's still ordered exactly like today — priority first. Nothing gets buried just for being far away.

  • A small "📍 Closest to you" strip sits above the full list, showing the 1–2 assigned jobs nearest to wherever the builder just stopped, with a distance badge — worked out the same way the Clocking page already estimates travel mileage.
  • It's a nudge, not a reorder: in the example here, the P1 Critical three miles away still sits above the routine job next door once you look at the full list below it.

10 End-of-day clock-out escalation

Before this feature, a builder still clocked in past 17:00 got one reminder push and nothing else happened — the shift stayed open until a manager manually closed it, however long that took. That manual path still works exactly as it did — a safety net was added on top:

  • The manager gets told too, not just the builder — and can act on it themselves, same as today.
  • Only if both of them ignore it past a configurable grace period does the system step in and close the shift itself — backdated to the 17:00 deadline, not whenever the auto-close actually ran.

5 Long breaks get flagged too — and show up live

Added after launch, alongside the required note on Unable to Do (step 4, Builder tab). Two more things happen automatically while a builder is on one of the timed short trips:

  • If the break runs past a configurable threshold (default 45 minutes) without them resuming, their manager gets a push — same idea as the end-of-day escalation, just for a single break instead of the whole day.
  • Where's the Team (Dashboard) and Today's Attendance (Clocking) both show exactly what the builder is doing and which job it's tied to, live — found and fixed the same day: these two views only ever checked for a clocked job or the older travel/break log, so a builder on one of these newer short trips was misread as simply "Available".

One grace period for everyone?

Built as a single global setting (Settings → Daily Attendance → "Auto clock-out grace period", default 2 hours), the same for every division. Whether it needs to vary by division or role was never revisited after launch — flag it if that turns out to matter in practice.
